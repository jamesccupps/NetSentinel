Audit your network

Checks your own networks when you ask: which devices answer, which risky services they leave open (Telnet, SMB, RDP, VNC, exposed databases, admin pages without TLS) and whether their certificates have expired. It only connects and reads what a service says first; it never tries a password. Use gentle mode for building or industrial controllers, which can fall over when scanned quickly.

Audits are off. Turn them on in the desktop app: Settings → Network audit.

Scan files for leaks

Drop files, folders or zips here. Logs, configs, documents, exports and packet captures all work. You get every IP address, domain, URL, email and MAC address in them, and any password, key or token that looks leaked, masked, with the file and line. Nothing leaves this computer: the files are scanned by NetSentinel here and deleted afterwards.
